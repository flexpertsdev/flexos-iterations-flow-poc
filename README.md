# FlexOS iterations flow (proof of concept)

One page of a project's website, every version Flexi has made: a carousel and a time slider from oldest to newest,
Fly through, Make current, notes pinned to a version, and five lenses on the page (Preview, Brief, Design, Plumbing,
Compare). Plumbing lists everything on the page, where it comes from, who writes it and when, and flags anything with
no source as INVENTED. Sample data: Crumb & Co, a made-up bakery.

Pulled as-is from its Vercel deployment (project `iteration-flow-prototype`, a Vercel Drop of 6 Oct 2026, live at
https://iteration-flow-prototype.vercel.app). `index.html` is the whole prototype, one self-contained page;
`vercel.json` is its deployment settings. Nothing was changed.
